Privacy Policy

Just a Scratchpad stores your note, text size, and appearance settings on your device. The app has no account sign-up, does not send notes to the developer’s servers, and contains no advertising or in-app analytics.

Import, copy, and share

Incoming text and links pass through local storage shared by the app and its share extension. When you choose to copy, share, open a link, or add an event to Calendar, content may be passed to iOS or the app you choose to complete that action.

Deleting notes and backups

You can clear your note in the app. Your iOS backups and copies shared to other apps are managed by those services.

Contact

Send privacy questions to support. We use the information you provide in a support request to respond and resolve the issue.
